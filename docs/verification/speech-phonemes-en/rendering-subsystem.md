## RenderingSubsystem Verification

### Verification Approach

The SpeechPhonemesEn RenderingSubsystem is verified by deterministic tests in
`test/DemaConsulting.Speech.Phonemes.En.Tests/RenderingSubsystemTests.cs` with the real phonemizer and renderers and the
real symbol tables of the two voice models: a trimmed copy of the Piper voice's `phoneme_id_map` (from its
configuration, CC BY 4.0, attributed in the file; the single committed copy is the Piper tests' and is linked into this
project) and Kokoro's own tokenizer file (linked from the Kokoro package). The subsystem-level tests
(`RenderingSubsystem_*`) check the two renderers together: vocabulary coverage proven exhaustively rather than by
example (every dictionary pronunciation, every sequence of three phones, the letter-to-sound conversions of a large
sample, every weak form and mark), and the conventions of both voices side by side on short texts. The units are
verified by their own `EspeakIpaRenderer_*`, `MisakiRenderer_*`, `WeakForms_*` and `PhonemeStream_*` tests in the same
file (see the unit verification documents), including the golden corpora of the two renderers.

The golden files `piper-ipa-golden.tsv` and `kokoro-misaki-golden.tsv` are regenerated by the tools command
`regenerate-goldens --renderers` and reviewed; they are regression baselines, not oracles. The agreement with espeak-ng
and misaki is measured locally by `measure-oracle` and recorded as aggregate numbers in
`docs/evaluation/phonemes-en/renderers.md`.

### Test Environment

- **Framework**: xUnit v3 running under the .NET SDK, on every target framework of the library
- **Execution**: `dotnet test` invoked by `build.ps1` and the CI pipeline
- **Project**: `test/DemaConsulting.Speech.Phonemes.En.Tests`
- **Files**: the golden files, the out-of-vocabulary golden words, the two symbol tables, copied next to the test
  assembly
- **Isolation**: no network, no model; the phonemizer and the renderers are immutable and shared by the tests of the
  class. The exhaustive coverage tests take several seconds

### Acceptance Criteria

The subsystem tests pass when: the inventories are the voices' own (159 Piper symbols, 115 Kokoro symbols); more than
130,000 dictionary pronunciations, all 69 x 69 x 69 phone sequences, more than 15,000 letter-to-sound conversions and
every weak form, mark and renderer mode render only to symbols of both tables; `Hello, world!` renders as
`həlˈoʊ, wˈɜːld!` for Piper and `həlˈO, wˈɜɹld!` for Kokoro; quotation marks and dashes are a pause for Piper and
Kokoro's own marks for Kokoro; an ellipsis is three dots for Piper and `…` for Kokoro; a hyphenated compound keeps every
stress for Piper and only the first for Kokoro; the flap, the linking r and the stress rules give the documented
strings; function words are weak only when weak forms are enabled (on by default for the Piper renderer, off by default
for the Kokoro renderer, which the Kokoro model package switches on; see *SpeechOnnxKokoro KokoroPhonemePipeline
Verification*);
and a long text is rendered as parts within the limit that lose nothing.

### Test Scenarios

#### Vocabulary coverage

**Tests**: `RenderingSubsystem_Inventories_AreTheModelsOwnSymbolTables`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Coverage_EveryDictionaryPronunciation_UsesOnlySymbolsOfBothModels`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Coverage_EveryPhoneInEveryNeighborhood_UsesOnlySymbolsOfBothModels`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Coverage_EveryOutOfVocabularyConversion_UsesOnlySymbolsOfBothModels`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Coverage_WeakFormWordsAndPunctuation_UseOnlySymbolsOfBothModels`

The set of symbols that the renderers can emit is a subset of each voice's table in every case; a symbol outside it is
reported by name. The precondition test shows that the tables are the real ones and not empty.

#### Conventions of the two voices

**Tests**: `RenderingSubsystem_Layout_WordsAndClausePunctuation_FollowTheModelConventions`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Layout_QuotesAndDashes_AreRenderedPerModel`,
`RenderingSubsystem_Layout_Ellipsis_IsRenderedPerModel`, `RenderingSubsystem_Compounds_StressIsPerModel`,
`RenderingSubsystem_SoundRules_FlapLinkingRAndStress_FollowTheModelConventions`

Short texts are rendered by both renderers and compared with the documented strings: `city` and `water` (flap),
`number is` (linking r for Piper only), `nobody` (no secondary stress next to the primary for Piper, kept for Kokoro),
`a well-known author`.

#### Weak forms

**Test**: `RenderingSubsystem_WeakForms_FunctionWords_AreUnstressedOnlyWhenEnabled`

`a cup of the tea` renders `ɐ kˈʌp ʌvðə tˈiː` for Piper with weak forms and `ɐ kˈʌp ˈʌv ðə tˈiː` without; the stress of
`of` stays for Kokoro by default and goes when weak forms are enabled.

#### Parts

**Test**: `RenderingSubsystem_Parts_LongText_StayWithinTheLimitAtClauseBoundariesWithoutLoss`

Forty sentences with a limit of 100 symbols give at least ten parts per renderer, each within the limit and ending at a
comma or a period except the last, together carrying every sound; a short text is one part equal to its plain rendering;
a text that is only an emoji has no part.

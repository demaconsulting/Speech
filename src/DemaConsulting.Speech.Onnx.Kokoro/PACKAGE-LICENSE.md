## Data Embedded in This Package

This package embeds two data files as assembly resources. The MIT License above does not cover
them; each is covered by the licenses described here.

- **`kokoro-en-lexicon.tsv.gz`**: a word-to-phoneme lexicon for English. Its word list is derived from the CMU
  Pronouncing Dictionary (CMUdict), which is covered by a BSD-style license that requires its copyright notice and
  conditions to be reproduced in binary distributions. Its phoneme strings were generated by running `misaki.en.G2P`
  (version 0.7.4) from the misaki project over those words. The misaki repository is distributed under Apache-2.0, and
  its English lookup reads misaki's own gold and silver dictionaries as shipped, so the lexicon can contain
  pronunciations taken from them; misaki does not document the upstream provenance of those dictionaries, and this
  package claims nothing about it beyond the repository's Apache-2.0 license (see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` for what its
  author has said, which has not been independently verified). This lexicon is scheduled to be replaced by one generated
  solely from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. This is a modified form of the original data: the word list was filtered
  to single-pronunciation alphabetic words plus apostrophe contractions, phonemized, and stored as a gzip-compressed
  tab-separated file. The CMUdict license text and the Apache-2.0 license text are in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
- **`kokoro-v1.0-tokenizer.json`**: the Kokoro v1.0 phoneme vocabulary (`tokenizer.json`),
  embedded verbatim from the `onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX` export of Kokoro-82M by
  hexgrad, which is licensed under Apache-2.0. The Apache-2.0 license text is in
  `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

The Kokoro model weights and voice files are not part of this package. They are downloaded on
demand, and their license is declared by the model itself through the `LicenseName` and
`LicenseUrl` properties of `ISpeechModel`.

See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` for the attribution, the full third-party license texts, and the
provenance of the embedded data.
